Dr Chen practises adult and paediatric dermatology, with subspecialty interests in skin cancer and pigmentary disorders.

Does Dr Chen see adults and children?

Yes. Dr Chen provides adult and paediatric dermatology consultations, covering medical, surgical, laser and cosmetic skin concerns.

Can I consult in Mandarin?

Yes. Consultations are available in English and Mandarin Chinese. You can also visit the 中文网站 .

How do I request an appointment?

Use the online appointment page , call 6223 4468, or contact WhatsApp on 8064 2866. For same-day or next-day availability, call or WhatsApp the clinic. Wait for the team to confirm your booking.

What should I bring?

Bring identification, current medication and skincare information, relevant referral letters or test results, and insurance details if applicable. Photographs showing changes in your skin may also be helpful.

Can I ask about fees before booking?

Yes. Contact the clinic for an estimate. Fees depend on your consultation and any tests or procedures. If you plan to use insurance, confirm your policy requirements and coverage before the visit.
