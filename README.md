# Dr Chen Dermatology Clinic — Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

Dr Chen Dermatology Clinic is a specialist practice at Collyer Quay Centre in Singapore’s Central Business District. Led by Consultant Dermatologist Dr Chen Qiping, it provides adult and paediatric care across medical, surgical, laser and cosmetic dermatology.

The website helps prospective patients explore care and arrange appointments. The redesigned homepage improves readability, simplifies care choices and provides clearer mobile contact options.

## Three Main Improvements

### 1. Larger Supporting Typography

Larger supporting text, clearer headings and a minimum text size of 15px replace labels and information areas previously below 15px. This improves readability and strengthens the distinction between headings and supporting details.

### 2. Consolidated Care Pathways

Six care pathways provide direct links while retaining access to both full condition and service directories. Previously, separate grids repeated related topics and lengthened the homepage. The consolidated overview reduces repetition and uses two columns on mobile.

### 3. Two Clear Mobile Contact Actions

Persistent, labelled “Call clinic” and “WhatsApp” buttons remain accessible while visitors scroll on mobile. Previously, the floating contact button prioritised WhatsApp, making calling less accessible. Patients can now choose either contact route directly.

## Design Improvements

- **Typography and hierarchy:** Larger text and clearer headings improve readability, with no text below 15px.
- **Layout:** Six pathways replace overlapping grids while preserving access to full directories.
- **Consistency:** Direct care links and explicit contact labels clarify destinations.
- **Mobile experience:** Two-column pathways and persistent contact buttons support browsing and enquiries.
